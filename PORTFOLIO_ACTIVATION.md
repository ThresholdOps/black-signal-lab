# Black Signal Lab Portfolio Activation

## Purpose

This document activates the closed Black Signal Lab public portfolio baseline v0.1 for real controlled portfolio use.

The v0.1 baseline remains closed and frozen.

Activation means the repository may now be deliberately linked, discussed, and used as evidence of the author's approach to controlled AI-assisted work.

Activation does not convert the repository into a product, open-source framework, reusable toolkit, official training program, or autonomous system.

## Activation Decision

```text
Decision: ACTIVATE BLACK SIGNAL LAB V0.1 AS AN ACTIVE PUBLIC PORTFOLIO.
```

Current operating state:

```text
Baseline: v0.1 closed and frozen
Portfolio use: active
Visibility: controlled public visibility
Reuse license: none selected
External contributions: not invited
Product claim: not approved
```

## Why Activate Now

The baseline has completed:

- method and governance definition,
- artifact lifecycle definition,
- review-material doctrine,
- human decision gate,
- governance vocabulary,
- three bounded public case studies,
- GitHub hygiene review,
- sensitive-content review,
- license decision,
- publication boundary,
- baseline closure review.

The repository is therefore coherent enough to be used as portfolio evidence rather than kept only as an internal preparation exercise.

## Approved Portfolio Framing

Use this framing:

```text
Black Signal Lab is my public portfolio repository for controlled AI-assisted work.

It shows how AI-supported workflows can be structured around reviewable artifacts, evidence, validation gates, source-of-truth boundaries, human review, and explicit decision ownership.

The repository uses public-safe documents, sanitized case studies, and synthetic examples. It is not a production system, open-source toolkit, or autonomous decision platform.
```

Short framing:

```text
Public portfolio for controlled AI-assisted work: artifacts, evidence, review gates, source-of-truth boundaries, and human decision ownership.
```

## Suitable Uses

The activated repository may be used for:

- AI governance discussion,
- process and operating-model discussion,

- private professional conversations,

## Boundaries That Remain in Force

Activation does not approve:

- open-source or open-content claims,
- contribution invitations,
- commercial packaging,
- product-launch framing,
- production-readiness claims,
- official training or certification claims,
- reusable-framework maturity claims,
- autonomous decision claims,
- disclosure of private project material.

## Link Checklist

Before sharing the repository:

- describe it as a public portfolio or method-inspection repository,
- mention controlled AI-assisted work,
- preserve the human decision ownership principle,
- avoid production and open-source claims,
- respect the no-license reuse boundary,
- do not imply that sanitized case studies expose private implementation,
- link to the repository with enough context to prevent misunderstanding.

## Activation Review Gate

A lightweight review is enough for:

- a portfolio link,
- a direct professional conversation,
- a short controlled public reference.

A stronger review remains required for:

- a formal launch campaign,
- conference or webinar use,
- a public methodology series,
- commercial service framing,
- training delivery,
- contribution invitation,
- a license change.

## Relationship to the Frozen Baseline

This activation is a BSL-02 decision.

It does not reopen BSL-01 or modify the substantive method baseline.

Future baseline expansion requires a separately approved, versioned effort such as v0.2.
